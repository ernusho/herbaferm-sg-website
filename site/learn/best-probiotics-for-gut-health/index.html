Best probiotics for gut health: what to look for before you buy

The best probiotic depends on the strain and the reason for taking it. A short guide to the questions that matter.

By Herbaferm editorial teamMedical reviewer: To supplyPublished 2 Nov 2026 (draft)

In short
  • A probiotic is a live microorganism given in an adequate amount that benefits the person taking it.[1]
  • Benefits are strain-specific and condition-specific; the AGA found evidence for only some uses.[2]
  • Herbaferm does not market its products as probiotics.

What is a probiotic?

The scientific definition is live microorganisms that, when administered in adequate amounts, confer a health benefit on the host.[1] Yoghurt with live cultures is not automatically a probiotic by this definition unless the organisms and amount are shown to benefit people.

Why does the strain matter?

Two bacteria with the same species name can behave differently. Evidence is tied to a named strain at a studied dose for a studied purpose. So "contains Lactobacillus" is not enough: look for the full strain name (for example, species plus a strain code) and whether it was tested for what you want it for.[1]

What do the guidelines say?

In 2020, the American Gastroenterological Association assessed probiotics across several digestive conditions. It recommended them in a small number of specific situations, suggested not using them routinely in others, and described the evidence in many areas as low quality.[2] If you have a medical condition, ask your doctor which, if any, applies to you.

How is a probiotic different from a prebiotic or postbiotic?

WordWhat it is
ProbioticLive microbes[1]
PrebioticFood for microbes already in your gut[3]
PostbioticNon-living microbes and their components[4]

Is Herbaferm a probiotic?

No. Herbaferm leaflets describe the products with the words "rebiotic" and "prebiotic", and which regulatory category fits in Singapore is still being confirmed. Until it is, we do not call any Herbaferm product a probiotic and we do not publish a live-organism count. The probiotic, prebiotic, postbiotic guide explains the terms further.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best probiotic for gut health?

It depends on the strain, the dose and the purpose. Evidence is strain-specific, and guidelines support probiotics only for certain uses.

Is Herbaferm Drops a probiotic?

Herbaferm does not market its products as probiotics. Its leaflets use the terms rebiotic and prebiotic, and the Singapore category is being confirmed.
