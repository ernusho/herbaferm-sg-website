Best supplements for gut health and weight loss: what is known, and what is not

The microbiome and body weight are linked in research. That does not mean a supplement will change your weight.

By Herbaferm editorial teamMedical reviewer: To supplyPublished 2 Nov 2026 (draft)

In short
  • Studies link the gut microbiome with body weight, but links are not proof that a supplement causes weight loss.[1]
  • Herbaferm does not claim any product helps with weight loss.
  • Fibre-rich whole foods have the most consistent evidence behind them.[2]

Is there a link between the gut microbiome and weight?

Researchers have found differences in gut microbial communities between people with and without obesity, and animal experiments show the microbiome can influence energy balance. Human findings are more mixed, and association does not show cause.[1]

Do supplements for gut health help with weight loss?

Showing that a particular product changes weight in people requires well-designed trials of that product. Marketing that pairs "gut health" with "weight loss" usually goes further than the research supports. Herbaferm does not make any weight-loss claim for its products, and under Singapore's rules, supplement claims must stay within what can be substantiated.[3]

What has the most consistent evidence?

Food. A series of reviews for the World Health Organization found that higher intake of dietary fibre and whole grains was associated with lower body weight and better health outcomes.[2] Diet also shifts the gut microbiome within days.[4] Practical steps: more vegetables, legumes and whole grains, fewer ultra-processed foods, regular activity and sleep. If weight is a health concern, see a doctor or dietitian.

Where could Fiber Elite fit?

Fiber Elite is a fibre-rich food additive that can be stirred into porridge, smoothies or yoghurt. It adds fibre to a meal; it is not a weight-loss product, and it contains gluten. See how to improve gut health for a food-first plan.

Frequently asked questions

Can gut health supplements help me lose weight?

There is no reliable evidence that a gut health supplement on its own leads to weight loss. Herbaferm makes no weight-loss claims.

What helps gut health and weight at the same time?

Fibre-rich whole foods, regular activity and sleep are the best-supported places to start. See a doctor or dietitian for personal advice.
