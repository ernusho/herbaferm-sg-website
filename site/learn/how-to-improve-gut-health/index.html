How to improve gut health: six evidence-informed habits

No quick fix, but plenty of simple habits that research supports. Start with food.

By Herbaferm editorial teamMedical reviewer: PendingPublished 2 Nov 2026 (draft)

In short
  • Diet is the strongest everyday lever on the gut microbiome that researchers have identified.[1][2]
  • A diet high in fermented foods increased microbiome diversity in a 10-week trial, while a high-fibre diet had more mixed effects.[3]
  • Persistent symptoms need a doctor, not a supplement.

1. Eat a wide range of plant foods

Vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, nuts and seeds feed different microbes. Dietary fibre intake is consistently linked with better health outcomes in large reviews.[4] Add new foods gradually and drink water as you raise fibre.

2. Include fermented foods you enjoy

In a 10-week Stanford trial, healthy adults who ate more fermented foods such as yoghurt, kefir, kimchi and kombucha showed greater microbiome diversity and lower markers of inflammation than a comparison group.[3] It was a small study and not a treatment trial, but it supports the food-first advice. More in fermented foods and gut health.

3. Notice how fast diet can change things

In a controlled study, switching to a diet of either mostly animal or mostly plant foods altered gut microbial communities within days.[1] Small, steady changes are enough to try; extreme diets are not needed.

4. Be thoughtful about antibiotics

Antibiotics can disrupt the gut microbial community, and in a study of repeated courses the community did not fully return to its starting state.[5] Take antibiotics only when prescribed and as directed, and ask your doctor about any questions on diet afterwards.

5. Sleep, stress and activity

These are part of general health advice and are discussed in reviews of diet and lifestyle on the microbiome.[2] They are worth keeping in mind even though the exact effects on the microbiome are still being studied.

6. Consider supplements last

Supplements can be an add-on once food and routine are in place. Use our checklist for choosing one. Herbaferm Drops, Fiber Elite and Prefer HF400 are food supplements that sit beside a varied diet. Our leaflets state that no health effects can be attributed to them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to improve gut health?

There is no instant fix. Research shows diet can shift the gut microbiome within days, and the usual advice is a wide variety of plant foods, fibre and fermented foods.

Do fermented foods improve gut health?

In one 10-week trial, a high fermented-food diet increased microbiome diversity in healthy adults. It was a small study, and results in other groups may differ.
